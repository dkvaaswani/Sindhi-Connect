How the calculator works

Costs

Each course's tuition, other fees and living costs grow with inflation from the published fee year to the year they are paid. Tuition and living costs have separate inflation rates. One-time costs fall in the first study year, and every study year is paid when it starts.

Savings

Savings grow at your assumed return. Monthly contributions are paid at month-end and rise each year by your chosen percentage. The monthly figure shown is the smallest amount that keeps each child's fund from running out in any study year.

Sources

Fees are taken from official university pages and averaged only when they share a country, course, student category and currency. Every figure links to its source on the Education plan step. The Excel version shows every formula.
