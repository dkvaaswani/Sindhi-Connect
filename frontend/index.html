About

A home for Sindhis, wherever they are.

From Sindh to the global diaspora, Sindhi Connect brings our language, stories, and people together on one platform.

  • Language

    Read, write, and speak Sindhi in both Arabic and Devanagari scripts, at your own pace.

  • Culture

    Explore Sufi poetry, music, festivals, food, and the traditions that make us who we are.

  • Community

    Meet Sindhis near you and across the world. Share stories, events, and opportunities.

Meet the person behind Sindhi Connect

Connecting People. Sharing Knowledge. Celebrating Sindhi Identity.

Dhanesh Kumar

Founder / Creator — Sindhi Connect

I believe connection creates understanding, and knowledge creates progress.

Sindhi Connect is my effort to bring people together through our language, culture, stories, knowledge, and shared heritage.

My goal is to create meaningful content that informs, inspires, and keeps our Sindhi identity alive for generations to come.

Our language. Our culture. Our stories. Our connection.
