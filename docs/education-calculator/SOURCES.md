## Exchange rates

## Known coverage gaps

- Pakistan: food, transport and personal living costs; public-sector medical college fees (only AKU is current).
- India: government medical colleges (e.g. AIIMS) – no official fee page reached; transport/personal living costs.
- USA: engineering differential fees at Kentucky/Purdue not verified; MD programmes are graduate-entry.
- UK: international Business Administration (Leeds 2026/27 'to be confirmed'); Leeds Medicine international fee to be confirmed; books/course costs.
- Australia: OSHC health cover and visa fee; second university for Commerce and Medicine.
- Germany: Accounting and Finance; KIT semester contribution; current RWTH semester contribution; TUM programme fee band.
- All countries: Dentistry, Pharmacy, Law, AI, Data Science, Cybersecurity and other programmes have no verified records yet.
- ACCA, CPA and CA: no verified records yet.
