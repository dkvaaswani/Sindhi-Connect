## Known gaps

- Status on 2 October 2026: every degree course offered in each country has a published fee except those listed here, which use clearly labelled planning estimates.
- India — fees for international (foreign-national) students: estimated at twice the Indian-national fee; only CHRIST University's international BBA fee is published in the dataset. Manipal's Foreign/NRI fee PDF could not be read.
- USA — Doctor of Physical Therapy (estimated from the PharmD fee); software engineering, artificial intelligence (computer science fee) and architecture (standard undergraduate rate); international fees for medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and law (non-resident rates assumed).
- Germany — nursing, data science, finance, and accounting and finance (state-set fees, so comparable programmes' fees are used).
- Pakistan — finance (business-school rate); FAST fees are worked out from the official per-credit rate and an assumed 33 credit hours a year.
- ACCA, CPA and CA everywhere — exam, registration and membership fees from each body's official fee list, spread over typical pathway length; tuition classes and review courses are not included.
- 'Other Qualification' everywhere — the average of the country's published non-medical degrees.
- Single-university benchmarks (one published fee only): several Australian courses (University of Queensland), most US health and law courses (University of Michigan), and several Indian courses (Manipal).
- Living costs — food, local transport and books in Pakistan and India, OSHC health cover in Australia and books in the UK are planning estimates; personal spending is not included anywhere.
- Visas — India's student-visa fee and Pakistan's for nationalities not in the official table are estimates; US reciprocity (issuance) fees that apply to some nationalities are not included.
- First travel and settling in — planning estimates by home region for every country.
- Fee increases — long published series exist only for England's fee cap, Australia's government student contributions and Pakistan's medical-college schedules; elsewhere one-year changes are recorded as evidence and a labelled planning rate is used.
- IIT Bombay's 2025-26 fee circular (records R021, R022) is no longer online at the recorded link.
- Singapore (added 15 October 2026) — physiotherapy uses the NUS Nursing fee (SIT's fee page could not be read); living costs are NUS's estimates read via the search listing; health insurance is an estimate.
- Malaysia — dentistry (from the Medicine fees), nursing (IMU fee from a course-search website) and physiotherapy (from health-science fees) are estimates; Malaysian students are shown private-university fees (public universities are much cheaper for citizens); books are an estimate.
- Ireland — software engineering, AI, data science and cybersecurity use the Computer Science fees; accounting and finance uses the Business fees; books are an estimate; Trinity's Medicine and Dental fees are its stated maximum.
- Qatar — Qatar University fees are per credit hour × 30 a year; software engineering, AI, data science and cybersecurity use the Computer Science fees; physiotherapy uses the Nursing rate; psychology is an estimate; local transport is an estimate. Qatari citizens are shown as paying no tuition.
- United Arab Emirates — American University of Sharjah's rate was read via its search listing; software engineering uses the Computer Science fee; health insurance and books are estimates; Emirati citizens are shown as paying no tuition.
